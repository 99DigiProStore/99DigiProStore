Excel for Finance Lab

Learn Excel by doing, not watching. You type real formulas on real finance data, such as GST invoices, EMIs, receivables ageing, payroll and project appraisal, and each answer is checked the moment you press Enter. It's one file that works offline.

What you get

  • 13 modules, from your first formula to a DCF valuation
  • 99 hands-on exercises on Indian finance data
  • Instant feedback that tells you exactly what's wrong, including a missing $ or a number typed where a cell reference should be
  • 10 finance case studies: loan schedule, depreciation, break-even, ratio analysis, GST invoice, receivables ageing, payroll, variance report, DCF and EOQ
  • 8 final challenges and a printable certificate of completion
  • 35 quiz questions on shortcuts, pivot tables, formatting, what-if analysis and error codes
  • A practice spreadsheet with sheets, formatting, filters, charts and pivot summaries. Open your own Excel or CSV file, and save your work as a real Excel file.
  • Formula suggestions as you type, and a guide to 104 functions with finance examples
  • Separate profiles with an optional PIN, so people sharing a device each keep their own progress

Topics covered

SUM to SUMPRODUCT, absolute and mixed references, IF, AND, OR, IFS, IFERROR, SUMIFS, COUNTIFS, VLOOKUP, XLOOKUP, INDEX/MATCH, text cleaning (GSTIN and PAN), dates (ageing, EMI dates, financial year), PMT, FV, PV, RATE, NPV, IRR and XIRR.

Who it's for

Commerce students, CA, CMA and CS students, freshers preparing for jobs, junior accountants and small business owners. It's a good fit for complete beginners too.
